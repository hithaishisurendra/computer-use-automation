Notes
-----
* EXIT: 0 for success AND business_outcome -- a business outcome is a legitimate
  answer, not a failure. 1=hard_failure, 2=caller_error, 3=auth_failure.
* "malformed input" never opened a browser: input validation runs against the
  artifact's declared pattern before Chromium starts.
* "fault: interstitial" recovered: the engine dismissed the overlay via its own
  Continue control and retried the step, then completed normally.
* "fault: slow_response" injects 6s per request. The search step's redirect chain
  therefore exceeds the checkpoint budget the artifact declares (8000ms), so the
  engine retries twice and then escalates. This is the artifact's declared budget
  being enforced, not an engine limit -- raising timeout_ms in the artifact would
  change this row.
* "cascade overlay/drift" runs the CASCADE artifact against the NORTHRIDGE server
  on purpose. Two independent drift signals fire and the run still completes:
    - app_version 4.2.3 recorded vs 4.2.1 observed (warning, not a failure)
    - member_ref_field's tenant-specific "Account Number" rung misses, so
      resolution falls through to the brittle role_ordinal rung -- recorded even
      though that rung succeeded.
  The business outcome is correct: a 10-digit account number is not a northridge
  member id, so no record matches.
